This Privacy Policy applies to all content, links, and interactions associated with the Sculpt & Glow brand online. By engaging with our content, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

Cookies

If you visit any website linked from our Sculpt & Glow profiles, cookies may be used. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us understand how visitors interact with our pages.

Types of cookies we may use:

  • Essential cookies — necessary for the website to function properly.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand traffic patterns and improve content. These are anonymised.
  • Preference cookies — remember your settings or language preferences.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect your browsing experience.
